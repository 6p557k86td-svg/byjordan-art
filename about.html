About

Jordan is a painter and digital artist whose work moves between the familiar and the surreal. Working in oil, acrylic, and digital media, Jordan brings vivid color and expressive marks to landscapes, flowers, figures, and everyday objects.

Across the collection, quiet scenes sit alongside strange, dreamlike worlds. Glowing skies, unexpected forms, and playful details give the paintings a mood that can feel tender, uneasy, or a little mischievous. Each piece offers an invitation to look closer and find your own story.
